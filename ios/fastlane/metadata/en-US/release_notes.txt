This is a new app, not an update to the old one.

Knock Knock is now a nightly speed-dating app. Every night from 7 to 8 PM Pacific, you get five-minute video dates with people within 75 miles. After each date you privately choose keep talking or pass, and a mutual yes opens a text chat.

The old calling, contacts, recents, and group calls are gone. So is the native call screen (CallKit) and the VoIP push that came with it.
